01 · Vehicle physics

Custom physics vehicle system

Cars that don't use Roblox constraints. Each wheel casts its own ray, solves its suspension and pushes real forces into the chassis. Turning comes from tire forces, so grip loss and drifting happen naturally.

  • Raycast suspension with spring and damper per wheel, tuned by damping ratio
  • Slip-angle tire grip using a simplified Pacejka curve
  • Ackermann steering with a speed-sensitive angle limit
  • Drivetrain: torque curve, 6-speed automatic, reverse, hill hold, drag and downforce
  • Feel: nose dive when braking, shift jolt, engine sound tied to RPM
local slipAngle = math.atan2(lateralSpeed, math.abs(longitudinalSpeed) + 0.5)
local grip = math.sin(shapeFactor * math.atan(stiffnessFactor * slipAngle))

02 · Live events

Music-synced sound system

A complete party venue. Players request songs, design the light show for their track and take over the DJ booth while it plays. Every light, speaker and effect reacts to the music.

  • Song requests by audio ID, validated with MarketplaceService: must exist, be audio and not already be queued
  • Loudness analysis: each song is measured for average and peak loudness so lights scale to it
  • Per-song light show: beat color, ambient color and intensity, smoke and fire colors
  • Requester becomes the DJ: their avatar is built and placed at the booth
  • Vote to skip when more than half the server agrees, with a default playlist fallback
  • Stage effects: pulsing speakers, moving spotlights, smoke and fire machines, light-up floor
  • Venue extras: NPC staff serving food and drinks, animated doors, bathroom occupancy
